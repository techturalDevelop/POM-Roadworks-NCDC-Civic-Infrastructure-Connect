MODULE 3: OFFICIAL PUBLIC NOTICES

Create a notice-management interface with:

Notice reference, title, category and issuing department.

Description, affected location, effective dates and expiry.

Supporting documents and map links.

Draft, under review, approved, scheduled, published, expired and archived states.

Drafting, review, approval, scheduling and publishing workflows.

Notice preview before publication.

Change history and publication timestamps.

Only authorized users may approve and publish official notices. Drafts must never appear as official public notices.
