MODULE 2: INFRASTRUCTURE ASSET REGISTER

Create a searchable asset register supporting:

Roads and road segments.

Drainage and culverts.

Bridges and structures.

Streetlights and traffic signs.

Other asset categories configurable by authorized administrators.

Each asset must support a unique ID, category, location, responsible authority, description, condition, inspection history, photographs, maintenance history and next inspection date.

Provide asset detail pages, map locations, filters and links to related projects, reports and work orders.

Do not fabricate engineering certification or actual NCDC asset data.
