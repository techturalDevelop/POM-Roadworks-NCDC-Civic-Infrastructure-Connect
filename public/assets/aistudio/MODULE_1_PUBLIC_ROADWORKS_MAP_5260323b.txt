MODULE 1: PUBLIC ROADWORKS MAP
Implement:
-OpenStreetMap-based interactive map.
-Color-coded markers for planned, active, completed and suspended roadworks.
-Separate visual indicators for impact or severity.
-Search by road, suburb, landmark and project reference.
-Filters by status, location, impact and date.
-Map and list view toggle.
-Roadwork detail panels.
-Work descriptions, schedules, last-updated timestamps and approved alternative-route information.
-Mobile-responsive layout and accessible map controls.
-Clear OpenStreetMap attribution.
-Use geographically plausible Port Moresby sample records, explicitly marked as unverified demonstration data.
